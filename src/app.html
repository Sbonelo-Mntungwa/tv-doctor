macOS is stopping TV Doctor from reaching your television. TV Doctor cannot see your router either, so this is a permission, not the set. Apps opened from Finder need permission to find devices on your network.

Open System Settings, Privacy & Security, Local Network and switch on TV Doctor, then run Examine again. If TV Doctor is not listed, quit and open it once more, then allow the prompt. Rebuilding with build.sh gives the app a new signature, so macOS may ask again.

The television is not answering. The network is fine from here, so the set is most likely in standby. Wake it with the remote, then run Examine again.
